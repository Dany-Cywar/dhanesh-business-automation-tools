# Dhanesh M — Business Automation & Data Tools

A portfolio of browser-based tools built for management reporting, document processing,
finance, HR workflows, OCR, operational analysis and administrative automation.

## Projects

### InstantDash
Excel/CSV dashboard builder with KPIs, filters, interactive charts, pivot analysis and data-quality review.

Folder: `instantdash/`

### Minutes Maker
Offline meeting-minutes tool that converts raw notes into structured minutes, decisions, owners and deadlines.

Folder: `minutes-maker/`

### PDF Toolkit
Browser-based PDF merge, split, extract, reorder, rotate and page-organization utility.

Folder: `pdf-toolkit/`

### SnapText
OCR tool for extracting text from screenshots, images and PDFs with crop, rotate and export controls.

Folder: `snaptext-ocr/`

### StampIt
Bulk PDF/image watermarking tool with text/logo modes, preview and configurable placement.

Folder: `stampit-watermark/`

### Net Worth Calculator
Tracks assets, liabilities, net worth, category breakdowns and financial trends.

Folder: `net-worth-calculator/`

### GST Invoice Generator
Creates GST invoices with tax calculations, preview, history and PDF export.

Folder: `gst-invoice-generator/`

### MatchPoint — Resume vs JD Analyser
Compares resumes with job descriptions and surfaces match scores, keywords and ATS-style recommendations.

Folder: `matchpoint-resume-jd-analyser/`

### MoneyDesk — Personal Finance Workbench
Budgeting, transaction tracking, savings analysis and personal-finance dashboarding.

Folder: `moneydesk/`

### StatementIQ — Bank & Credit Card Statement Analyser
Parses and analyses bank/credit-card statements using PDF extraction, OCR, Excel tooling and charts.

Folder: `statementiq/`

### SalaryLens — CTC Decoder & Offer Comparator
Decodes compensation packages, estimates take-home pay and compares salary offers.

Folder: `salarylens/`

## Technology
HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with project-specific browser libraries loaded through CDN where required.
